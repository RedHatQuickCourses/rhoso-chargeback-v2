In this module, we’ll cover the foundational steps for troubleshooting basic rating issues within your environment. To ensure accurate charge calculations and system performance, there are several key areas you need to verify.

First, you must ensure that all RHOSO Observability components are up and running correctly. As part of this, check that Ceilometer is enabled and functioning as expected. It is critical to confirm this, because any missing or incorrect metric data will directly lead to inaccurate charge calculations.

Next, verify that Prometheus is operational. You’ll need to confirm that it is successfully scraping metrics from the RHOSO Observability components. To do this, check that all required metric sources are being captured correctly through your `ScrapeConfig` resources.

You should also confirm that Loki is fully operational and capable of receiving data streams from the RHOSO Observability components.

Once you have verified the status of your observability stack, move on to inspecting the CloudKitty status and its configuration. Examine both the logs and the status messages of the Rating components for any errors. Pay close attention to any clear error messages, as these often point directly to specific problems such as uninstalled dependencies or underlying configuration issues.

Finally, keep in mind that we have provided extra hands-on training exercises to help you practice verifying the status of these components and troubleshooting any issues you might encounter. Following these verification steps systematically is the best way to maintain a healthy and accurate rating environment.
