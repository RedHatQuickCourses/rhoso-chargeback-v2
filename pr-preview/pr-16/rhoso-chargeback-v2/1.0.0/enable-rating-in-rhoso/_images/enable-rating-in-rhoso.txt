In this module, we will discuss how to enable the Rating service within Red Hat OpenStack Services on OpenShift, also known as RHOSO. 

Starting with RHOSO 18.0 Feature Release 5, you can utilize chargeback capabilities by enabling the Rating service, which is powered by CloudKitty, directly within the Telemetry Operator. 

Before you begin, there are a few important prerequisites to keep in mind. You must have an operational RHOSO deployment with Telemetry already enabled. Additionally, you must have a configured `MetricStorage` and the Loki Operator successfully deployed in your environment. 

Once these prerequisites are met, activating the service is straightforward. You can turn on CloudKitty directly through the `OpenStackControlPlane` Custom Resource.
