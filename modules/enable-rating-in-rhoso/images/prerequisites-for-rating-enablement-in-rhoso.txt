To successfully deploy and activate the Rating service in your Red Hat OpenStack Services on OpenShift, or RHOSO, environment, there are five essential prerequisites you must meet. Let’s walk through them.

First, you must have a fully functional and stable RHOSO environment. Before proceeding, you should verify the health of your deployment by checking the status of your control plane and data plane instances using the OpenShift command line. You can confirm that the OpenStack Control Plane is ready by checking that the status condition message reads "Setup Complete." Additionally, ensure that all OpenStack operators are running and that the OpenStack Data Plane deployment has successfully finished. 

Second, you must ensure that Ceilometer is enabled. The Telemetry Operator is the primary interface for managing the CloudKitty service, so it is vital that the Telemetry service is active, Ceilometer is enabled, and a MetricStorage solution is in place. You can verify this by checking the OpenStack Control Plane specification to confirm that both Telemetry and Ceilometer are enabled, and by running commands to verify that both are deployed and in a ready state.

Third, confirm that Metric Storage, specifically Prometheus, is enabled. Because CloudKitty relies on collected metrics to perform its rating calculations, Prometheus serves as the primary data storage in RHOSO. You should verify that the MetricStorage is enabled in your configuration and that the Prometheus instance is fully deployed and ready to receive data.

Fourth, you must have a Loki instance available. Once CloudKitty retrieves metrics and applies its rating rules, it stores the resulting data in Loki. Ensure that a Loki instance is designated for CloudKitty’s use and is accessible. You can verify this by confirming the installation of the Loki operator and verifying that the LokiStack has been correctly created.

Finally, you must update the global OpenStack secret with the necessary CloudKitty credentials. This ensures secure access for the CloudKitty service pods. You can confirm this is configured correctly by inspecting your `osp-secret` to verify that it contains the required CloudKitty credentials.

By ensuring these five prerequisites are met—a healthy RHOSO environment, enabled Telemetry and Ceilometer services, an active Prometheus MetricStorage, a functional Loki instance, and updated secret credentials—you will be ready to successfully deploy and activate the Rating service.
