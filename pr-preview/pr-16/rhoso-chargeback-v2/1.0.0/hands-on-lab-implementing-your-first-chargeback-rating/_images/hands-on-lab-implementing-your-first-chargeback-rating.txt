Welcome to this hands-on lab on implementing your first chargeback rating. In this session, you will gain practical experience by configuring and using the Rating service.

To complete the activities, you will be using Ansible playbooks available in the lab repository, which you can find linked in the supporting documentation. Throughout this lab, we will focus on three key objectives.

First, you will set up your lab environment for chargeback. This involves installing all required dependencies and enabling the Rating service within your RHOSO environment.

Second, you will create and apply a rating rule. Specifically, you will define a rule for a compute flavor using the hashmap module.

Finally, you will verify and analyze your consumption data by validating that your rating configuration is working as expected.

Let’s get started by preparing your environment.
