To begin your training, you will first need to set up your lab environment on the Red Hat Demo Platform, or RHDP. Please follow these steps to launch your lab.

First, log in to the RHDP portal. Once you are logged in, search for the catalog entry labeled "FIXME Lab Catalog Name." When the entry appears in your search results, click on it to open the catalog page. From there, click the "Order" button.

On the order form, you will need to fill in the required details. Before you finalize your order, please carefully review the warning provided at the bottom of the form. Once you have read it, be sure to check the box labeled, "I confirm that I understand the above warnings." Finally, click the "Order" button again to submit your request.

A few important notes to keep in mind: the lab environment may take approximately "FIXME minutes" to become fully provisioned and ready for use. Once it is ready, you will receive an email containing your access details. If you don’t see the email, remember that you can always retrieve your lab access information directly through the RHDP portal.

To access your lab via the portal, simply click on the "Services" option located in the left-hand menu. From the list that appears on the right-hand side of the page, select your lab to view your specific access details.

If you need to access the portal itself, please refer to the links provided in your documentation. Red Hat associates should navigate to demo.redhat.com, while Red Hat partners should use partner.demo.redhat.com.
